How to read this
Tip: chance each team wins the opening tip from the two jumpers' jump-ball record. Scores first: from the tip, team strength and the spread.
Model: team scores first x the player's share (shot volume and his record of first baskets as a starter). Chance: 40% model, 60% market (each book's prices normalised to 100%, averaged). Free throws count.
Grade (pick grade): how strong the play is at the best price. Edge = chance minus the break-even chance of the price. A = 4+ pts over break-even or 8%+ expected return; B = 2-4 pts or 4-8%; C = smaller positive edge.
Starting lineups are projected from the last game and the injury report (they confirm ~30 min before tip).
