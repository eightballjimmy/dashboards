How to read this
1+ pts / 2+ pts: chance the player records at least 1 / 2 points if he plays. Props are usually void if he does not dress; Play is the chance he dresses.
Projections combine recency-weighted, shrunk scoring rates (even strength per 60 + power play per game), projected ice time (recent games blended with DailyFaceoff line slot), team goals from the market total and moneyline, opposing goalie, home/away and back-to-backs.
