How to read this
Chance: model probability blended with the no-vig market (average of the books). Fair: the price that matches that chance. Edge: expected return at the best price.
Grade (pick grade): how strong the play is at the best price. Edge = chance minus the break-even chance of the price. A = 4+ pts over break-even or 8%+ expected return; B = 2-4 pts or 4-8%; C = smaller positive edge.
Model line: player-based team ratings (current rosters and projected minutes, injuries from the official NBA report), rest, back-to-backs, travel and altitude.
