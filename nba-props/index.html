How to read this
Chance: 30% model, 70% no-vig market (average over books at that line). Model: projected minutes (injury report and rotation) x per-minute rates x pace x opponent x teammate usage, negative-binomial distribution at the line.
Grade: A = player and key teammates cleared, settled role; B = role uncertainty (new team, few games this season, a teammate is a game-time decision, big minute swings); C = player is questionable/doubtful or has little NBA history.
SUSPECT: the model and the market disagree by a lot (usually a lineup or data issue): shown, never ranked.
